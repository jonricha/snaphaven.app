Frequently Asked Questions

Do my photos ever leave my local home network?

No! SnapHaven operates entirely peer-to-peer between your mobile device and home server over local Wi-Fi. Your photos are never uploaded to any third-party servers.

What operating systems are supported for the server?

SnapHaven Server currently supports Windows 10 & 11 (with system tray integration and auto-updates). macOS and Linux builds are currently in active development.

Are original quality photo metadata and EXIF preserved?

Yes! SnapHaven performs byte-for-byte transfers of your original files, preserving EXIF metadata, camera settings, GPS location, and video bitrate.
